Privacy Policy for ManagerOS

Last updated: September 28, 2026

ManagerOS is a project management and productivity application designed to help users organize projects, tasks, milestones, risks, meetings, reports, priorities, and related management activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ManagerOS, including its AI-assisted features.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

ManagerOS stores project and management information on your device so that the application can provide its core functionality. Depending on how you use the app, this information may include:

  • Projects and project details
  • Tasks and milestones
  • Risk and ROAM information
  • SWOT information
  • Meeting notes and related records
  • Project reports and planning information
  • Deadlines, dates, priorities, and management notes
  • AI Manager conversation information maintained by the application

Core project information is maintained locally by the application unless a feature requires information to be transmitted to provide that feature, such as an AI-assisted request or a sharing action initiated by you.

2. AI-Assisted Features

ManagerOS includes AI-assisted management features. When you choose to use an AI feature, information necessary to process your request may be transmitted from the app to ManagerOS's backend infrastructure and AI processing service.

Depending on the feature you use, this information may include the text you enter and relevant project context needed to generate a useful response, such as project details, tasks, milestones, risks, meeting information, priorities, or other management information associated with your request.

Important: You should not enter passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers, medical information, or other highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts or project fields unless it is necessary and appropriate for your use of the app.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through ManagerOS is used to provide and operate features requested by the user, including:

  • Managing projects and related records
  • Displaying project information and analytics
  • Generating AI-assisted management guidance
  • Supporting project planning and prioritization
  • Supporting risk analysis and management workflows
  • Generating or preparing project-related information requested by the user

4. Sharing Features

ManagerOS may allow you to share or export information using sharing options available on your device. Sharing occurs only when you initiate the relevant sharing action.

Information you choose to share may then be handled by the application, service, or recipient you select. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices.

5. Notifications

ManagerOS may provide local notifications and reminders for supported project activities, such as due dates or meetings, when those features are enabled by you. Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.

6. Advertising and Tracking

ManagerOS does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

7. Sale of Personal Information

ManagerOS does not sell users' personal information.

8. Third-Party Services

ManagerOS relies on service providers where necessary to operate certain functionality, including cloud infrastructure and AI processing services. Information transmitted to such services is limited to information required to provide the feature requested by the user.

9. Data Control

ManagerOS provides functionality for users to manage project records within the application, including supported editing, archiving, and deletion functions.

Removing the application from your device may remove locally stored application data in accordance with the behavior of your operating system and device backups.

10. Children's Privacy

ManagerOS is a general project management and productivity application and is not specifically designed or marketed for children.

11. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to support the security of information processed through ManagerOS. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ManagerOS evolves or as legal, regulatory, or operational requirements change. The current version will be made available at this page, together with its latest update date.
